FAQ

Where is your catering menu & how can I order?

Our catering menu is not online and is available exclusively by phone or in person. Call us today at +1 (708) 653-0009 to discuss catering!

Is outside food allowed?

Unfortunately, outside food is not allowed. Cakes and desserts are allowed for celebration purposes only (no consumption is allowed).

Should I reserve a table before coming?

Reservations are highly recommended. We retain the right to cancel or not accept reservations if you're late or haven't confirmed, and to not accept reservations on certain days of the year.
