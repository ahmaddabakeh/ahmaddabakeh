Al-Bahaar's Global Customer Privacy Statement

Our Policy

This Privacy Policy pertains to the information collected by Albahaar Inc. (referred to as “Al Bahaar,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) when you visit our websites, utilize our associated mobile applications (“Apps”), or connect to the WiFi in our establishments (collectively, the “Services”). It outlines the collection, use, and sharing of your information, your options regarding its use, how we protect such data, and how you can contact us regarding our privacy practices.

Information You Provide

We may acquire information directly from you through our Services when you create an account, place an order, make a reservation, sign up for special offers, or interact with our platforms. This may include personally identifiable details such as your name, address, email, phone number, date of birth, company affiliation, payment information, or geolocation.

Information Collected Automatically

We also gather certain information automatically through tools like cookies and web server logs when you interact with our Services. This may include IP addresses, device identifiers, software details, browser characteristics, and pages viewed. You can adjust your browser settings to manage cookies, but disabling them may affect your experience on our sites.

Our service providers may furnish us with information you provide to them, such as when ordering catering, making reservations, or using our restaurant WiFi networks. We may combine this information with data we have about you. Please review the terms and privacy policies of these third-party services, as we are not responsible for their practices.

Use of Personal Information

We may use the personal information we collect for various purposes, including personalizing user experiences, processing transactions, marketing, and improving our Services. We may also create de-identified records for analysis and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Sharing of Personal Information

We may share your personal information with our subsidiaries, business partners, and vendors who provide services on our behalf. In the event of a business transaction, we reserve the right to disclose information obtained through the Services.

Analytics and Advertising

We utilize third-party technology for interest-based advertising and web analytics services. You can manage your preferences regarding data collection for advertising purposes.

Social Networks

Certain features of our Services may allow interactions with third-party services. Review the terms and settings of these services to learn more about their data practices.

Sweepstakes, Contests, and Other Promotions

Participation in promotions may require the submission of personal information, which may be disclosed to other parties involved.

Linked Websites

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to linked websites operated by third parties. Review the privacy policies of these websites for their practices.
