The Ismail Family

The Iconic Father & Son Team

The Ismail father and son team epitomize the essence of hard work, dedication, and family values in the restaurant industry. Led by Khalil Ismail, affectionately known as “Abu Faris,” this family's journey in the culinary world began in 1987 when Khalil opened his first pizza shop. His relentless determination and passion for Middle Eastern cuisine led him to establish his prized possession, Al Bawadi Grill, in 2008.

Despite facing numerous challenges, Khalil persevered, transforming Al Bawadi Grill into an iconic Middle Eastern restaurant in Bridgeview. Over the years, his three sons – Faris, Hamza, and Saif – joined the family business, starting as busboys, hosts, and waiters and gradually working their way up to managerial roles. For Khalil, it was crucial that his sons earned their positions through hard work rather than simply inheriting them.

In 2020, Khalil opened a second location in Niles, which quickly became another Middle Eastern staple. Buoyed by that success, he went on to launch two additional restaurants: Al-Bahaar and Chef Eyad.

The Ismail family's story is a testament to the power of family bonds and the value of hard work — building thriving businesses and lasting legacies within the communities they serve.
