What we heard

The brief

A TikTok Live shopping brand for recognisable makeup, skincare and haircare. It should feel like a real beauty brand first: bold, premium and social-native, never cheap or like a discount store.

boldpremiumfemininesocial-native

Success: loud on TikTok Live, recognisable as a 48 px profile picture, works in one colour, sits naturally next to Sephora and Ulta brands.

Final design

Beauty Rush Bold

Heavy black capitals on candy pink: simple, loud and instantly recognisable on a TikTok Live.

  • Type-only wordmark in the spirit of the big beauty houses: confident, no clip-art
  • Candy pink and black own the feed and read at a glance on Live graphics
  • BR monogram carries the brand where the full name is too small
